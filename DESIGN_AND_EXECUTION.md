## 1. High-Concept Pitch
**Shift Alchemist** is a 3D isometric action roguelite set aboard a derelict industrial freighter overrun by procedurally mutated Xenomorphs. Armed with a **Pressurized Fluid Nozzle**, a **Vacuum Siphon**, a **Fluid-Plumbed Jetpack**, and a **Local Gravity-Shift Pulse**, the player manipulates a real-time **10 cm (`0.1 m`) 3D cellular automata and ballistic particle sandbox** of **Water, Oil, Cryo-Coolant, Acid, Fire, Steam, Electricity, Wood, Ice, Glass, and Steel**—turning the ship's failing gravity and volatile cargo into kinetic and elemental chain reactions modeled after *Noita* and *Teardown*.

## 3. Canonical 4-State World Model & Volume-Only Conservation Ledger

### 3.1 The Four Canonical Representations
1. **Bit-Packed Static/Cellular Voxel Grid (`Uint32Array(3,686,400)`):**
   * Stores all static/carvable geometry and grid-resident fluids/gases (`ID 0..12`) at `0.1 m` (`10 cm`) resolution (`1 cell = 1.0 L`).
   * **Glass Vats = Pure Grid Geometry:** A `GLASS_VAT` is a hollow 3D shell of `GLASS_VAT` voxels enclosing actual `WATER`, `OIL`, or `CRYO` voxels directly inside the `Uint32Array`. No separate vat inventory exists—shattering the shell exposes enclosed fluid cells directly to gravity and radial shatter impulses.
2. **Pre-Allocated Ballistic Quanta Pool (`ballisticQuanta` SoA, Max `4,096`):**
   * Zero-GC typed arrays (`x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, packedVoxelUint32, flightTicks, fuseTicks, flags, active`; `flags` bit 0 = `igniteInFlight`, bit 1 = `firedByPlayer` (nozzle tap/hold and dash exhaust), bit 2 = `primed` (§9 *Phase-Lock Fuse*); `fuseTicks: Uint16Array`). Each active quantum represents exactly `1.0 L` in continuous 3D motion. A quantum's `tempByte` and `chargeUnits` are fixed at spawn (no in-flight conduction or charge decay).
   * **Allowed Ballistic Materials:** `WATER`, `OIL`, `CRYO`, `ICE` (shattered ice shards), and `ACID`. (`STEAM` and `FIRE` are grid-only.)
   * **Ballistic Phase Transitions (Stage 3, evaluated at impact/landing, writing the impact cell if `AIR`, else `lastAirCell` if `AIR`, else routing the product through `placeOrDeferFluid()`; the only in-flight case is a quantum with `igniteInFlight` set—only the *Spark-Plug Igniter* sets it—which applies the `OIL` rule at `flightTicks === 2`):** *(Owner decision 2026-11-19.)* Transition-on-impact is the **base rule for every fluid**: enemy and Boss napalm cannot ignite at their own mouths, and the player's hot water no longer flashes beside the nozzle. Changing this for the player is a **progression upgrade**, not a fixed rule. The *Phase-Lock Fuse* track delays the impact transition of `firedByPlayer` quanta (rank 3 adds a manual trigger), and the *Hazard Lining* track makes the player immune to their own blasts, burning, shock stun and embrittlement. Both are specified in §9 under Hazard Tracks. Enemy and Boss fluid always uses the base rule.
     * Superheated `OIL` (`tempByte >= 95` / `+180°C`) -> writes `FIRE` via `transitionCell()`, increments `V_oil_combusted`, deactivates.
     * Superheated `WATER` (`tempByte >= 75` / `+100°C`) -> writes `STEAM` (`tempByte = 75`, `timer = 45`) via `transitionCell()`, queues a Phase Explosion trigger at that cell, deactivates (conserved in `V_water_total`).
     * Warmed `CRYO` (`tempByte >= 50` / `0°C`) -> sublimates to `AIR`, increments `V_cryo_boiled_off`, deactivates (and queues a Phase Explosion trigger if `tempByte >= 75` / `+100°C`).
3. **Continuous 3D AABB Dynamic Entities (`entities: Entity[]`, Max `32`):**
   * Budgeted as **`1` Player + `<= 12` Aliens (including Boss) + `<= 16` Crates (`3` spare)** (`4–12` crates spawned per combat/side room: `1` Bio-Cache per combat room, `2` per optional salvage side-room). Entities collide against solid grid voxels and one another via swept AABB collision and never translate as multi-voxel grid blocks.
   * **Unified Effective Mass Contract:**
     `effectiveMassKg = dryMassKg + tankFluidMassKg + glandFluidMassKg`
     where `fluidMassKg = liters * MaterialSpec[matId].densityKgPerL`. Exo-suit servos hold player `WASD` run speed constant across tank fill levels, while `effectiveMassKg` governs knockback resistance, entity-entity collisions, fluid impact impulse, and Wall-Splat damage.
   * **Canonical Entity Schema:**
     `{ id, kind ('player'|'alien'|'crate'), x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, halfW, height, halfD, upSign (1|-1), dryMassKg, hp, maxHp, tempByte, chargeUnits, burnTicks, wetTicks, stunTicks, stunImmunityTicks, dashTimer, dashGraceTimer, impactIFrames, impulseTimer, reflectCooldown, locomotion, glands: Array<{mat, liters, capL, tempByte, chargeUnits}>, carapace, deathHusk }`
     *(Note: The player's back-tank is a player-only singleton `WorldState.playerTank`, referenced when computing player `effectiveMassKg`, rather than stored in `Entity.glands[]`.)*
     *(Alien/runtime fields, integers in ticks unless noted, serialized with the room: `variantId`, `aiState`, `attackCooldownTicks`, `telegraphTicks`, `activeGlandIdx`, `offNavTicks`, `pathNodes` (`Int16Array` of nav-node ids, recomputed after load), `bossPhase` (`1|2`). `hp` is a JS `number`: DOTs subtract `dps / 60` per tick and the HUD shows `Math.ceil(hp)`. Non-Boss aliens have exactly one gland and at most one carapace (`carapace: 'none' | 'thermal' | 'insulated' | 'spined'`).)*
   * **Coordinate & `-1G` Foot Convention:** `pos.y` is strictly AABB minimum Y (`bottom`). Ground checks, stair step-ups, tank displacement/backfire spills (there are no passive boot spills), and plumb-line origins compute **`feetY = upSign === 1 ? pos.y : pos.y + height`**.
   * **Entity-Fluid Sampling & Thermal Recovery Policy:** Entities are **non-displacing status samplers with movement drag** (`WOOD_CRATE` props are **dynamic obstacles/projectiles, never walkable platforms**, and receive full-AABB water-voxel overlap buoyant lift in `WATER`, since a hollow `60 kg`, `1 m³` crate has bulk density `0.06 kg/L < 1.0 kg/L`: `a_buoy = min(2.2*g, (waterVoxels / 1000) * 2.5*g)` with `0.85/tick` drag and `maxVy = 3.5 m/s`). Each tick (Stage 8), entities sample exactly two cells: the feet cell at (AABB center `x`/`z`, `feetY + 0.05 m * upSign`) and the torso-center cell—never the supporting solid underfoot, so standing on electrified or hot steel does not shock/burn by itself (fluid on that steel does): `OIL` applies `0.6x` ground speed; `WATER` sets `wetTicks = 120` (`2.0 s`—extinguishing `burnTicks = 0`, granting fire-ignition immunity while `> 0`, and increasing electrical shock damage by `1.25x`), pulls entity `tempByte` `12°C` (`3` bytes) per `15 Hz` tick toward the water's `tempByte` (one-way entity thermal exchange—water cells are not reciprocally heated/cooled by entities; cools hot targets and thaws cold/embrittled targets toward `+20°C`), and applies `0.75x` drag at `>= 0.4 m` depth; `FIRE` sets `burnTicks = 180` (`3.0 s`, `12 DPS`) if `wetTicks === 0`; electrified cells apply `18 DPS` (`22.5 DPS` if `wetTicks > 0`) + `0.75 s` stun (subject to the Stun Contract, §8.2); `CRYO` applies `8 DPS` and chills `tempByte` by `-12°C` (`-3` bytes) per `15 Hz` tick toward `-196°C` (embrittling at `<= -48°C` / byte `38`, while warming the sampled `CRYO` cell `+4°C` / `+1` byte); **`ACID` applies `20 DPS` entity corrosion damage**. Both `wetTicks` and `burnTicks` decrement by `1` per `60 Hz` tick (clamped at `0`) whenever the entity is not actively overlapping `WATER` or `FIRE` respectively. At **`1 Hz`** (`(simTick % 60) === 0`), any entity not touching `CRYO` or `FIRE` relaxes `tempByte` by `1` byte (`4°C`) toward ambient `55` (`+20°C`).
4. **Discrete Fluid Inventories (`WorldState.playerTank` & `alien.glands[]`):**
   * Store exact integer liters (`liters`), `capL`, `materialId`, `tempByte`, and `chargeUnits`. Mixing sets `chargeUnits = max(existing, incoming)`; a tank chamber's `chargeUnits` decays `-2`/tick in Stage 1 like grid charge, while a gland's `chargeUnits` is constant (a *Bio-Voltaic* gland stays at `60`; decay applies only after its fluid leaves the alien). Fired/spilled liters carry the chamber's or gland's current `tempByte`/`chargeUnits`. Glands never regenerate.
   * *Starter Unbaffled Tank (`120 + 30 * rank` L -> `120 / 150 / 180 / 210 L`):* Low-pressure tank with an **air headspace** (thermally insulated from ambient room air, but contains oxygen—**any** fill or mix, including an empty-chamber first fill, that results in `WATER` `tempByte >= 75` (`>= +100°C`) or `OIL` `tempByte >= 95` (`>= +180°C`) triggers an immediate **Tank Backfire**). Siphoning electrified `WATER` (`chargeUnits > 0`) into a Starter chamber that already holds a **different** material also triggers an immediate backfire (the air headspace + voltage arc is the stated cause); the same fill into an empty Starter chamber or a same-material `WATER` chamber does not.
   * *Baffled Dual-Chamber Tank (`2 x (90 + 15 * rank)` L -> `180 / 210 / 240 / 270 L`) & Alien Glands:* **Inert-gas pressurized (safe up to and including `+240°C` / byte `110`) and thermally insulated** (not vacuum, so `+200°C` liquids do not boil, and oxygen-free, so `+200°C` Pyro-Napalm cannot ignite until exiting into room `AIR`). **Any** fill or mix, including an empty-chamber first fill, that results in `tempByte > 110` (`>= +244°C`, byte `>= 111`) triggers a **Tank Backfire**, and cross-material Cryo Thermal Shock remains an independent backfire trigger.
   * *Mixing Temperature & Cross-Material Cryo Shock:* Adding fluid of the same material (or filling an empty chamber) computes the volume-weighted integer `tempByte` average: in the low-pressure Starter Tank, `WATER` averaging `>= 75` (`>= +100°C`) or `OIL` averaging `>= 95` (`>= +180°C`) triggers a **Tank Backfire**; in pressurized Baffled chambers, same-material mixing is safe through byte `110` (`+240°C` inclusive) and only backfires above it (`tempByte > 110` / `>= +244°C` / byte `>= 111`). In **both** tanks, siphoning `CRYO` into a non-empty chamber holding fluid at `>= +100°C`—or siphoning `>= +100°C` fluid into a non-empty chamber holding `CRYO`—triggers an immediate **Cryo Thermal-Shock Backfire** instead of a displacement spill.

### 3.2 Volume-Only Conservation Ledgers (Zero Hidden Sinks)
This engine strictly conserves **discrete `1.0 L` volumetric quanta** across four disjoint fluid ledgers. **Ledger scope:** each ledger is evaluated over the resident room plus the player's tank (`Gland(mat)` includes `deathHusk` glands). While a room is resident each `V_*_total` must stay constant tick to tick; at room entry it is recomputed as (restored room terms + tank), and the `node:test` invariant is "room terms at exit equal room terms at the next entry". The `F3` overlay shows the resident-room totals and their drift since entry (must be `0`). **Physical mass is NOT conserved across phase changes** (`1 L` of `WATER` becoming `1 L` of `ICE` or `STEAM` preserves `1:1` cell volume). Structural solids (`GLASS_VAT`, `WOOD_BARRIER`, `HULL_STEEL`, `STEEL_GRATE`, `BOUNDARY_HULL`) exist outside the fluid ledgers (shattering/carving removes them to `AIR` with viewer-only debris; burning `WOOD_BARRIER` spawns `FIRE` without modifying `V_oil_combusted`). **`ICE` is not a structural solid for this purpose:** it is a water-ledger phase, so `ACID` can never carve an `ICE` cell away (doing so would delete conserved water volume); `ICE` leaves the grid only by melting `1:1` to `WATER` or shattering `1:1` to `Ballistic(ICE)`.

1. **Water Cycle Ledger (100% Closed-Loop Invariant):**
   `V_water_total = Grid(WATER + ICE + STEAM) + Ballistic(WATER + ICE) + Deferred(WATER + ICE + STEAM) + Tank(WATER) + Gland(WATER)`
   * `WATER <-> ICE`: Freezes at `T <= 0°C` (byte `50`), melts at **`T >= +4°C` (byte `51`)** everywhere (explicit `4°C` hysteresis band). Once frozen, `ICE` is a static solid voxel (no buoyancy rule).
   * `ICE` Shatter: Converts `1:1` into `Ballistic(ICE)`; on landing, re-inserts as `ICE` if `tempByte <= 50` or melts `1:1` to `WATER` if `tempByte >= 51`.
   * `WATER -> STEAM`: Flashes `1:1` at `T >= +100°C` (byte `75`), spawning `STEAM` at **`+100°C` (byte `75`)** and queuing one Phase Explosion trigger at that cell (subject to Stage 6 coalescing). When `STEAM`'s `15 Hz` timer reaches `0` OR it touches a solid `<= +80°C` (byte `70`), it condenses `1:1` back to `WATER` with **`tempByte = Math.min(tempByte, 70)`** (preventing instant re-boil).
2. **Oil Ledger (Decoupled from Wood Fire):**
   `V_oil_total = Grid(OIL) + Ballistic(OIL) + Deferred(OIL) + Tank(OIL) + Gland(OIL) + Sac(OIL) + V_oil_combusted` (`Sac(mat)` is the Boss's Brood Sac, §8.3, and is `0` outside the Boss Sanctum)
   * `OIL` ignites **only** when `T >= +180°C` (byte `95`) **and** at least one 6-neighbor is `AIR`. On ignition, `OIL -> FIRE` immediately increments `V_oil_combusted`.
   * **Pairwise Fire Dousing Rule:** In Stage 5(c), each `WATER` cell with `steppedThisPass === 0` douses at most **one** adjacent `FIRE` cell with `steppedThisPass === 0`: `WATER` transitions to `STEAM` (`+100°C`, staying in `V_water_total`; this reaction-produced steam does **not** queue a Phase Explosion—only temperature-driven boiling does), that `FIRE` cell transitions to `AIR`, both set `steppedThisPass = 1` and are **appended to `stage5Touched`** for Stage 5(i) cleanup, then `break`.
3. **Cryo Ledger:**
   `V_cryo_total = Grid(CRYO) + Ballistic(CRYO) + Deferred(CRYO) + Tank(CRYO) + Gland(CRYO) + Sac(CRYO) + Emitter(CRYO) + V_cryo_boiled_off`
   * `Emitter(CRYO)` is the *Leaking Cryo Pipe*'s finite room reserve (starts at `500 L`, decremented `1:1` when `placeOrDeferFluid()` accepts a liter into the grid, `ballisticQuanta`, or `deferredFluidQueue`; if placement is blocked and the helper returns `false`, the reserve is unchanged and retries next tick, and the reserve is serialized/restored with emitter state). It is a real ledger term, never a hidden source.
   * **Exact Finite Cryo Thermal Paths (No Generic Conduction):**
     1. Direct contact with any cell, emitter, or entity at **`T >= +100°C` (byte `75`)** -> instant **Cryo Boil-Off Phase Explosion** (`-> AIR`, `V_cryo_boiled_off++`), evaluated before gradual warming.
     2. Adjacent `WATER` -> freezes `1` `WATER` cell to `ICE` (`0°C`) per `15 Hz` tick, warming that `CRYO` cell `+32°C` (`+8` bytes) until `T >= 0°C` (`50`) peaceful sublimation (`-> AIR`, `V_cryo_boiled_off++`).
     3. Overlapping entity or adjacent `WOOD_BARRIER` -> chills target `-12°C` (`-3` bytes) per `15 Hz` tick toward `-196°C`, warming that `CRYO` cell `+4°C` (`+1` byte) per `15 Hz` tick until `T >= 0°C` sublimation (`-> AIR`, `V_cryo_boiled_off++`).
     4. At **`1 Hz`** (`(simTick % 60) === 0`), any `CRYO` cell 6-adjacent to a non-`GLASS_VAT`, non-`CRYO`, non-`AIR` solid (`HULL_STEEL`, `STEEL_GRATE`, `ICE`, `WOOD_BARRIER`) warms `+4°C` (`+1` byte)/s (`~49 s` max floor puddle lifetime before `0°C` sublimation). Intact `CRYO` `GLASS_VAT` shells stay insulated indefinitely until broken, and the *Leaking Cryo Pipe* emitter draws from its finite `500 L` `Emitter(CRYO)` reserve (counted in `V_cryo_total`; emission decrements the reserve `1:1` only when `placeOrDeferFluid()` accepts the liter).
     5. Floating `0G` `CRYO` with no solid contact warms `+4°C` (`+1` byte) every `5.0 s` (`(simTick % 300) === 0`, `~4 min` max to `0°C` sublimation).
4. **Boss Acid Ledger:**
   `V_acid_total = Grid(ACID) + Ballistic(ACID) + Deferred(ACID) + Gland(ACID) + Sac(ACID) + V_acid_consumed` (siphon rejects `ACID`, so no `Tank(ACID)` term exists).
5. **Zero-Sink Transfer Guarantees:**
   * *Siphon Cap & Source Guard:* `take = Math.min(2 + siphonRank, activeChamber.capL - activeChamber.liters, availableSourceLiters)`, where `availableSourceLiters` is the exact count of eligible grid fluid cells (or eligible stunned/`< 50%` HP alien gland liters, or a Boss gland during a Gorge, §8.3; never the Brood Sac) inside the `6.0 m`, `35°` LOS cone. **One material per tick:** the tick's material is that of the nearest eligible source (`distSq`, then `idx`; a gland counts at its alien's torso center; for the multi-gland Boss, the gland matching the active chamber's material first, else the lowest-index non-empty non-`ACID` gland), and only sources of that material count toward `availableSourceLiters`; the compatibility / displacement / backfire check (§8.2) runs atomically **before** any liter is extracted. Each extracted liter strictly decrements its exact source cell or gland 1-by-1 up to `take`—never overflows the active chamber or overdraws source. Siphon intake fills **only the active chamber**; a full active chamber stops intake (press `R` to switch) rather than auto-switching. Each extraction wakes the cell's 6-neighbors so the resulting void refills deterministically.
   * *Total Placement Helper (`placeOrDeferFluid`) & `deferredFluidQueue`:* Every off-grid -> grid placement (ballistic reinsertion, tank displacement/backfire spills, alien gland bursts, emitter injection) routes through one total function: (1) target cell -> radius-2 spiral -> expanding Manhattan-shell BFS (`r = 3..12` outside entity AABB, after the radius-2 spiral), (2) if `r = 12` is exhausted, promote to `ballisticQuanta` only for tank/gland/emitter transfers (an already-ballistic reinsertion skips this step and defers or stays active), (3) if `ballisticQuanta` is also full, append to the room's **`deferredFluidQueue`**. A tank/gland/emitter promotion in step (2) spawns at the target cell with `v = 0`. Placing `ICE` never targets a cell overlapping an entity AABB (the search continues outward), so no entity is ever embedded in a new solid. A multi-liter spill may run one shared BFS and fill free cells in BFS order (one deterministic search instead of one per liter). Each deferred entry is a fixed `{ mat, tempByte, chargeUnits, targetX, targetY, targetZ }` record with `MAX_DEFERRED_FLUID = 4,096` (`~48 KB`, serialized inside the `32 MB` snapshot cap); retries run `16` items/tick, re-check the current entity AABBs before placement, and count in `Deferred(mat)` on the ledger until an `AIR` cell opens. Every successful placement wakes the destination cell + its 6 neighbors and routes through `markCellDirty()` (so `dirtySnapshot`, `dirtySolidMesh` for reinserted `ICE`, and `markFluidRenderDirty()` all agree). When `deferredFluidQueue` is full, `placeOrDeferFluid()` returns `false` and **never deletes a liter**: new tank displacement siphons are **cleanly blocked with the old chamber preserved** (an atomic capacity check runs before any liter leaves the old chamber, so there is no partial two-material chamber), ballistic reinsertion leaves the quantum active and retries next tick, alien gland payloads persist on a non-colliding death husk (§3.2), and the airlock transition holds closed until it can place or serialize the remaining ballistics (§4.4). Otherwise incompatible tank displacement is **guaranteed to empty the old chamber to `0 L`** before accepting the new material (zero hidden sinks, zero two-material chambers). Spilled `+200°C` Pyro-Napalm ignites in `AIR` and increments `V_oil_combusted`. For vat shatters and explosions, a full `ballisticQuanta` pool simply leaves remaining cells on the grid to pour out via the `60 Hz` CA.
   * *Starter -> Baffled Auto-Split (Zero Overflow at All Ranks):* The shared Tank Cap rank (`0..3`) governs both starter and baffled capacities, so the starter can never out-rank the baffled chambers; ranks carry over automatically (`toA = Math.min(liters, 90 + 15 * rank)`, `toB = liters - toA`). Even at max rank `3` (`210 L` full starter), `toA = 135 L` and `toB = 75 L <= 135 L` (`60 L` spare headroom in Chamber B)—strictly zero overflow at every rank.
   * *Alien Death Gland Burst:* On alien death, all remaining `gland.liters` spawn at torso center as `ballisticQuanta`, falling back cleanly through `placeOrDeferFluid()` (`r = 3..12` -> `deferredFluidQueue`) if the ballistic pool is full. If the ballistic pool **and** deferred queue are both full, the alien's entity record persists as a non-colliding, non-siphonable `deathHusk` (`hp = 0`, `deathHusk = true`) holding its remaining `glands[]`; each tick retries placement until fully drained, then the husk is removed. Because it reuses the alien's own entity slot, `MAX_ENTITIES` is unchanged, and husks never count as hostiles for airlock locks, AI, or wave-clear checks. No gland liter is ever deleted.

## 4. Core Architecture, Bit-Packing, Chunking & Performance Contract

### 4.1 Strict Separation of Concerns & Seeded Determinism
* `src/core/`: Pure TypeScript simulation state, `balance.ts`, bit-packed 3D CA, pooled SoA ballistics, graph-grammar deck generator, per-dirty-chunk RLE snapshots, coarse `3`-layer A* AI, and swept AABB physics. **Zero Three.js or DOM imports.**
* `src/viewer/`: Three.js isometric cutaway renderer (`3` `THREE.BatchedMesh` solid buckets + `1` opaque/alpha-tested rim-tinted fluid pass + `1` 3D overlay pass + `1` directional shadow pass = `6` scene passes), fragment-shader sightline X-ray cutaway, cosmetic siphon ribbon, HTML corner HUD, `F3` Debug Overlay, and async snapshot compression/IndexedDB spill store (`snapshotStore.ts`).
* **Seeded Deterministic PRNG & Global `simTick`:** `simTick` is a **global monotonic run counter** (never reset on airlock room transitions, so suit cooldowns/timers persist seamlessly across rooms), and each room's `roomSeed` is immutable across backtracking. All CA tie-breaks, `slideDir` assignments, initial non-`AIR` voxel `shimmer` (`hash32(roomSeed, 0, idx) & 7`), `0G` isotropic gas steps, and radial impulse jitter use `hash32(roomSeed, simTick, idx)`—guaranteeing bit-exact replays and invariant tests.
* **Determinism Rules for `src/core/`:** `hash32(a, b, c)` is a fixed integer mix (e.g. murmur3 `fmix32` over `a ^ Math.imul(b, 0x9E3779B1) ^ Math.imul(c, 0x85EBCA77)`) defined once in `src/core/hash.ts`. Sim code uses only `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt`, `Math.imul`, `Math.trunc/floor/round/min/max/abs` (IEEE-exact everywhere); it never calls `Math.sin/cos/tan/atan2/exp/log/pow` or `Math.random` (not bit-identical across browsers)—unit vectors come from `hash32` lattice directions normalized with `Math.sqrt`, squares are written as multiplications, and aim directions arrive from the viewer already normalized and quantized to `1/1024`. `runSeed` is taken from `Date.now() >>> 0` at run start (or a `?seed=` URL parameter for replays/tests) and `roomSeed = hash32(runSeed, roomId, 0)`. A replay is `{ version, runSeed, simLod }` + one input record per tick (key bitmask + quantized aim); recording it is a dev tool, not a shipped feature.
* **State Ownership (`WorldState = RunState + resident RoomState`):** `RunState` (survives airlocks, cleared on death/new run): `runSeed`, deck graph, `simTick`, player `Entity` record, `playerTank`, the `6` upgrade-track ranks (`4` base + `fuseRank` + `liningRank`), owned attachments, Baffled flag, `scrap`, active chamber, gravity-pulse cooldown/timers, per-room snapshot index (`snapshotStore`). `RoomState` (resident room only; serialized at exit, restored at entry): grid, non-player `entities[]`, `deferredFluidQueue`, fallback ballistic records, emitter states, vat registry, workbench usage, lock state + failsafe timers, room gravity mode, `cleared` flag. Everything else (active lists, bitsets, overflow flags/cursors, `explosionQueue`, ballistic pool, render caches, nav-grid) is derived/transient and rebuilt by the Room-Load Reset Contract.

### 4.3 32-Bit Voxel Layout, Exact `4°C` Bytes & `transitionCell()` Normalization
Every cell in `Uint32Array(3,686,400)` packs:
* **Bits 0–7 (8 bits): `MaterialID` (`0..12`)**
* **Bits 8–15 (8 bits): `Quantized Temperature` (`0..255`)**
  * Formula: `T_C = (byte * 4) - 200` (`byte = (T_C + 200) >> 2`), exact `4°C` steps: `-200°C` (`0`), `-196°C` (`1`, `CRYO`), `-48°C` (`38`, Embrittled), `0°C` (`50`, `WATER -> ICE` freeze & `CRYO` sublimate), `+4°C` (`51`, `ICE -> WATER` melt), `+20°C` (`55`, Ambient), `+80°C` (`70`, `STEAM -> WATER` condense), `+100°C` (`75`, `WATER -> STEAM` boil & `CRYO` shockwave), `+180°C` (`95`, `OIL` ignite), `+200°C` (`100`, Pyro-Napalm), `+220°C` (`105`, `WOOD` ignite), `+300°C` (`125`, Thermal Vent), `+648°C` (`212`, `FIRE`), `+820°C` (`255`, max).
* **Bits 16–21 (6 bits): `Timer / Electrical Charge` (`0..63`)**
  * `FIRE` / `STEAM`: Decrements on the **`15 Hz` cellular clock** (`(simTick & 3) === 0`), `0..63` = `0..4.20 s`.
  * Conductors (`WATER`, `HULL_STEEL`, `STEEL_GRATE`, `ACID`): Electrical `chargeUnits` in `2 V` steps (`0..63` -> `0..126 V`, `60 units = +120 V`), decaying by `2` units per `60 Hz` sim tick (`30` frames = `0.5 s` visible pulse).
* **Bits 22–31 (10 bits): `Flags & Metadata`**
  * Bit `22`: `steppedThisPass` (always `0` at the start of every Stage 4 sub-pass and at the start of Stage 5; set to `1` when moved, reacted, or newly transitioned; **cleared to `0` across all visited/touched cells at the end of EVERY Stage 4 sub-pass AND by the end-of-tick clear that runs on EVERY tick (end of Stage 8, §6.1)**, because transitions in Stages 3, 6, 7 and 8 also set it on ticks where Stage 5 does not run). Bit 22 is transient: it is set/cleared with raw bit operations, the one sanctioned exception to the `writeCell()` choke point.
  * Bits `23–25`: `shimmer` (`0..7` *Noita* color variant, initialized deterministically via `hash32(roomSeed, 0, idx) & 7`).
  * Bits `26–27`: `slideDir` (`0 = +X`, `1 = -X`, `2 = +Z`, `3 = -Z`).
  * Bits `28–31`: `damage` (`0..15` solid structural wear).
* **`transitionCell(idx, newMat, newTempByte)` Normalization Contract:**
  * `-> ICE`: `tempByte = Math.min(newTempByte, 50)` (never leave `ICE` above `0°C`), `timerOrCharge = 0`, `damage = 0`, `slideDir = 0`.
  * `-> STEAM`: `timerOrCharge = 45` (`3.0 s` at 15 Hz), `tempByte = 75` (`+100°C`), `damage = 0`, `slideDir = 0`.
  * `-> WATER` (from `ICE`/`STEAM`): `timerOrCharge = 0`, `tempByte = Math.max(newTempByte, 51)` if from `ICE` (melt hysteresis), `tempByte = Math.min(newTempByte, 70)` if from `STEAM`, `damage = 0`, `slideDir = hash32(roomSeed, simTick, idx) & 3`.
  * `-> FIRE`: `timerOrCharge = 30` (`2.0 s` at 15 Hz), `tempByte = 212` (`+648°C`), `damage = 0`, `slideDir = 0`.
  * `-> AIR`: `tempByte = 55` (`+20°C`), `timerOrCharge = 0`, `damage = 0`, `slideDir = 0`, `shimmer = 0`.
  * All transitions clamp `tempByte` to `0..255` and `timerOrCharge` to `0..63`; `transitionCell()` is the only sanctioned path that **converts** a cell's `MaterialID` in place (phase change, reaction, carve, shatter). Stage 4 moves/swaps (`moveCell()` / `swapCells()`, built on `writeCell()`) and `placeOrDeferFluid()` relocate whole cell values without normalization; all three paths call `markCellDirty()`.
  * All transitions **except `-> AIR`** preserve `shimmer`, and a transition or placement **out of** `AIR` sets `shimmer = hash32(roomSeed, 0, idx) & 7` (shimmer is cosmetic and is not required to match after a reload); all transitions set `steppedThisPass = 1` (appending `idx` to `stage5Touched` so Stage 5(h) skips birth-tick decay and Stage 5(i) clears bit 22 before next tick's Stage 4), call **`markCellDirty(idx)`** (setting `dirtySnapshot = true`, `dirtySolidMesh = true` if solid occupancy changed, and `markFluidRenderDirty(chunkIdx)` per the trigger contract), **explicitly update `activeThermalSolids` membership** (unsetting `thermalBitset` when leaving a conductive/thermal solid such as `ICE -> WATER`, `WOOD_BARRIER -> FIRE`, or `-> AIR`; enqueuing when entering an **eligible** thermal solid—`HULL_STEEL`, `STEEL_GRATE`, `ICE`, or `WOOD_BARRIER` that is 6-adjacent to a non-solid or within `2` voxels of a heat/electric source (a `FIRE` cell or a Hazard Emitter footprint)—with `tempByte` off its relaxation target or `chargeUnits > 0`), and apply the **chunk halo**: when the cell lies on a chunk face (`(x & 15) === 0 || (x & 15) === 15`, same for `y`, `z`), the face-adjacent chunk(s) are also marked `dirtySolidMesh` / `markFluidRenderDirty()` under the same conditions, because greedy faces and 6-face interior culling there read this cell.

### 4.4 Active-Cell Overflow, Persistent Fluid Render Cache, `48 MB` `BatchedMesh` & `32 MB` RLE
* **Bitset-Deduplicated Active Lists & Separate Age-Ordered Overflow Scanners:**
  * `activeCellsA / activeCellsB: Int32Array(16,384)` deduplicated in $O(1)$ via **two** bitsets, `activeBitsetCur` / `activeBitsetNext: Uint32Array(115,200)` (`460.8 KB` each): `Cur` answers "is this cell in this tick's list" (Stage 5(b) `isActive`), `Next` deduplicates pushes into next tick's list—one bitset cannot do both. Separate `activeThermalSolidsA / B: Int32Array(8,192)` + `thermalBitsetCur` / `thermalBitsetNext` (`460.8 KB` each) track thermal/electrified solids, but **member eligibility is restricted** to `HULL_STEEL` / `STEEL_GRATE` / `ICE` / `WOOD_BARRIER` cells that are exposed (at least one 6-neighbor is non-solid) or within `2` voxels of a heat/electric source (added via `transitionCell()` or heating/electrification when eligible with `tempByte` off its relaxation target or `chargeUnits > 0`; dropped on list swap whenever no longer eligible OR once at its relaxation target with `chargeUnits === 0`). A push into a full list never evicts a member: it sets the cell's chunk in `fluidOverflowFlag` / `thermalOverflowFlag` instead. Both bitsets clear strictly in $O(\text{active})$ by unsetting only the pushed indices at list swap (`< 0.02 ms`).
  * **List Swap & Keep-Awake Contract:** the swap runs once per tick at the start of Stage 4: `cur <- next` for both lists (and bitsets), the new fluid list is sorted ascending with `Int32Array.prototype.sort` (Stage 4 order), and `next` is emptied. A cell enters `next` only by being woken (`markCellDirty()` on itself or a neighbor, re-seeding, overflow rescans) or by **keeping itself awake** when visited, which it does iff any of: (1) it is `FIRE` or `STEAM` (timers); (2) `chargeUnits > 0` (decay); (3) its `tempByte` is off its relaxation target (`55`; `ICE` only when `< 50`; never `CRYO`/`GLASS_VAT`/`BOUNDARY_HULL`); (4) it is `CRYO` with an active warming path (§3.2 paths 2–5); (5) it moved this tick. A visited cell meeting none of these sleeps (`0` CPU). Wakes skip `AIR`, `BOUNDARY_HULL`, `GLASS_VAT`, and solids that are not eligible thermal members.
  * Separate **`fluidOverflowFlag`** and **`thermalOverflowFlag`** arrays per chunk feed two independent age-prioritized scanners (`fluidOverflowCursor` and `thermalOverflowCursor`), each scanning up to **`2` oldest overflow chunks/tick** (the flag arrays are `Int32Array(900)` holding the `simTick` at which the flag was set, `0` = clear; oldest = smallest, ties broken by distance to the player, then chunk index; stopping early if the target list fills) so neither fluid nor thermal solids starve—zero deleted cells. The scanners are simulation state and run at the end of Stage 8 on every tick, never in per-frame render prep.
* **6 Scene Passes, `48 MB` LRU `BatchedMesh` & Persistent Fluid Render Cache (Sleeping Fluids Always Render):**
  * Solid greedy meshes pack into **`3` `THREE.BatchedMesh` buckets** (Opaque Solids, Transparent Glass/Ice, Permeable Grates—with screen-space sightline X-ray cutaway in the BatchedMesh fragment shader), using zero-GC scratch buffers and a distance-to-player-sorted remesh queue (`max 16/frame`). The Glass/Ice bucket sets `sortObjects = true` for correct alpha ordering. Greedy meshing **ignores `damage` bits** (cracks render via lightweight overlay decals on damaged cells, rebuilt for chunks flagged by `dirtyDecals` and capped at `MAX_DAMAGE_DECALS = 512` nearest to the player). Geometry memory is capped at **`48 MB`** with **distance-prioritized LRU eviction** of farthest chunk meshes if hit (re-meshed automatically upon player approach).
  * **Persistent Shared-Pool Fluid Render Cache (Decoupled from `activeCells`):** One fixed `fluidCachePool: Float32Array(24,576 * 4)` (`393 KB`, counted inside the `129 MB` budget) holds every cached `(x, y, z, paletteIndex)` surface cube. `chunkFluidOffset: Int32Array(900)` and `chunkFluidCount: Uint16Array(900)` slice the pool per chunk, `dirtyFluidRender: Uint8Array(900)` marks rebuilds, and `dirtyDecals: Uint8Array(900)` marks damage-decal rebuilds. Only chunks marked by `markFluidRenderDirty(chunkIdx)` re-scan their `16³` cells using **6-face same-fluid interior culling** (cubes surrounded on all 6 faces by the same fluid skip instancing, cutting an intact `2,500 L` vat to `~910–1,000` surface cubes, or `~3,600–4,000` across `4` vats); **sleeping fluid chunks use `0` simulation CPU and `0` cache-rebuild CPU and remain rendered indefinitely** (the per-frame copy is `O(visible <= 28,672)`). Rebuilds are distance-prioritized and capped at **`MAX_FLUID_CACHE_REBUILDS_PER_FRAME = 16`** (`8` under rendering fallback). **Pool allocation:** a rebuilt slice is written in place when its new count fits its previous slice length; otherwise it is appended at the pool tail; if the tail would pass `24,576`, the pool is compacted once (stable, by chunk index, `<= 393 KB` memmove) and the append retried; if it still does not fit, the farthest chunks' slices are dropped (count `0`, left `dirtyFluidRender = true`) until it fits—this is the "farthest chunks visually cull first" rule.
  * **`markFluidRenderDirty(chunkIdx)` Trigger Contract:** Explicitly call it on (a) Stage 4 moves (source **and** destination chunks), (b) `transitionCell()` whenever `oldMat` or `newMat` is in `{WATER, OIL, CRYO, STEAM, FIRE, ACID}` and the material actually changes, (c) `placeOrDeferFluid()`, (d) vat shatter, explosion, and free-fall promotions, (e) atomic siphon extraction, (f) room load, (h) the **chunk halo**: any of (a)–(g) on a cell at a chunk face also marks the face-adjacent chunk (§4.3), since that chunk's 6-face interior culling reads the cell, and (g) **the `chargeUnits > 0` electrified-bit flip on a fluid/gas conductor** (BFS pulse, spark consumption, or `60 Hz` decay crossing `0`), because palette bit `128` (electrified) changes without changing `MaterialID`; charge decay that leaves the cell electrified does not rebuild the cache. No other path may add, remove, move, or re-electrify a grid fluid/gas cell without marking every affected chunk. Ballistic `ICE` is pool-driven and needs no chunk rebuild; placing a reinserted `ICE` voxel also marks `dirtySolidMesh`.
  * **Grid Write & Snapshot Dirty Contract (single choke point):** Every Stage 1–8 grid write that changes a persisted cell bit (material, `tempByte`, `timerOrCharge`, `slideDir`, or `damage`) must go through `writeCell()` / `transitionCell()` / `placeOrDeferFluid()` and call **`markCellDirty(idx)`**, which: (1) sets `dirtySnapshot[chunkIdx] = true`; (2) sets `dirtySolidMesh[chunkIdx] = true` if solid occupancy changed and `dirtyDecals[chunkIdx] = true` if `damage` changed; (3) calls `markFluidRenderDirty(chunkIdx)` only when `oldMat !== newMat` and either material is in `{WATER, OIL, CRYO, STEAM, FIRE, ACID}`, or when the electrified palette bit (`chargeUnits > 0`) flips (temperature/timer-only writes and `slideDir` updates still set `dirtySnapshot` but do not rebuild the fluid cache, because `paletteIndex` encodes only material, `shimmer`, and electrification); and (4) wakes the written cell plus its 6 neighbors into the next active list (saturating into `fluidOverflowFlag` / `thermalOverflowFlag` rather than dropping them); a Stage 4 move additionally wakes the **26-neighborhood of the vacated source cell**, because diagonal collapse (Rule 2) and `N_same` cohesion read diagonal neighbors that a 6-neighbor wake would leave asleep. This contract explicitly covers Stage 4 moves, ballistic promotions/reinsertion, ballistic phase transitions, siphon extraction, deferred placement, timer/charge decay, BFS pulses, structural damage, `ACID` carving, vat shatter, explosions, tank/backfire spills, and emitter injection. Bit `22` (`steppedThisPass`) is transient and never sets `dirtySnapshot`; bits `23–25` (`shimmer`) are regenerated on decode. `dirtySnapshot` is cleared **only** after RLE encoding or after a room load applies the saved snapshot — never by a normal write. Direct `grid[idx] = ...` writes outside this choke point are forbidden.
  * Up to `24,576` cached visible grid fluid/gas cubes (if exceeded, farthest chunks visually cull first without deleting simulation volume) + `4,096` ballistic quanta pack into **1 Instanced Fluid pass** (`Float32Array(28,672 * 4)` = `458 KB`, storing `(x, y, z, paletteIndex)` where `paletteIndex = (matId * 8) + shimmer + (isElectrified ? 128 : 0)` in `0.0..223.0`—zero `NaN` risk), rendered as **opaque/alpha-tested rim-tinted cubes** (no expensive 20k transparent sorting). The instance pass carries grid IDs `1–5` and `11` (`WATER`, `OIL`, `CRYO`, `STEAM`, `FIRE`, `ACID`) **plus ballistic `ICE` (`6`) shards**; static grid `ICE` is meshed in the Glass/Ice solid bucket and is **never** instanced by the fluid cull. Ballistic `ICE` shares the same 4-float path with an opaque shard palette entry (`paletteIndex <= 183`, inside the packed range).
  * Plumb-line, surface ring, cosmetic siphon ribbon, damage decals, and lock-on reticle render in **1 3D Overlay pass**, plus **1 Directional Shadow pass** (`6` total scene passes + HTML corner HUD & `F3` Debug Overlay). Damage decals are capped at **`MAX_DAMAGE_DECALS = 512`** (nearest to the player; farthest retire first, re-marking `dirtyDecals` on the retired chunk so it rebuilds on approach).
* **Discrete Airlock Persistence & `32 MB` Snapshot Cap (RLE + Deflate + `IndexedDB` Spill):**
  * Each chunk maintains a `dirtySnapshot` flag separate from `dirtySolidMesh`. Prior to RLE encoding in `src/core/`, only bits `22–25` (`steppedThisPass`, `shimmer`) are masked out (**`val & 0xFC3FFFFF`**, **persisting bits `26–27` `slideDir` and `28–31` `damage`** for exact determinism across backtracking); on decode, `shimmer` regenerates deterministically via `hash32(roomSeed, 0, idx) & 7` **strictly for `mat !== 0`** (`AIR` stays `0`).
  * **Paused Atomic Transition (Zero Race Conditions):** During the `21`-frame (`0.35 s` minimum) physical airlock door-cycle animation, room simulation pauses (`simPaused = true`) while: (1) all `ballisticQuanta` auto-reinsert into the grid via `placeOrDeferFluid()`; if the deferred queue is full and a quantum cannot be placed, it remains active and is serialized as a fixed SoA record with the room snapshot (restored on load), so the door never opens with a live quantum unaccounted for; (2) only `dirtySnapshot` `16³` chunks RLE-encode/decode at a bounded **`max 64 chunks/frame`** (`<= 2.5 ms/frame`, pre-allocated `<= 4 MB` scratch buffer) with **zero-delta pruning only** (chunks bit-identical to `roomSeed` are omitted; modified chunks are **never evicted back to `roomSeed`**; pruning is optional and, if implemented, must compare persisted bits exactly against a copy of the freshly generated chunk—never a hash—and must **delete** that chunk's previously stored entry, otherwise a stale snapshot would be restored; v1 default: no pruning, every dirty chunk is re-encoded and replaces its old entry), (3) `dirtySnapshot` clears on encoded chunks, and (4) non-player `entities[]` (including `deathHusk` payloads), `deferredFluidQueue`, fallback ballistic records, emitter states (including the Cryo Pipe `500 L` `Emitter(CRYO)` reserve), workbench terminal usage, airlock lock states, and room gravity serialize (normalizing timed `C`/`V` pulses to `1G` on entry). If the encode + decode + mesh work does not fit in the `21` frames (e.g. more than `21 x 64 = 1,344` chunks to encode/decode), a placement/serialization fallback cannot complete, or an async `IndexedDB` reload exceeds `0.35 s`, the visual blast door simply **holds closed (`simPaused = true`)** until complete.
  * **`32 MB` Post-Compression Snapshot Ceiling (Hard via `IndexedDB`; Best-Effort if Unavailable):** `32 MB` post-compression in-RAM snapshot budget, within a combined `~129 MB` JS/CPU + geometry budget (`14.75 MB` grid + `0.39 MB` shared fluid cache pool + `48 MB` mesh cap + `32 MB` snapshots + `4 MB` scratch + `~30 MB` scene/JS, which also covers the `1.84 MB` of active/thermal bitsets and the nav/decal arrays), leaving `~220 MB` headroom for browser/GPU/WebGL under the **`< 350 MB` total browser tab process target**). In `src/viewer/snapshotStore.ts`, when in-RAM snapshots exceed `24 MB`, least-recently-visited room snapshots compress via `CompressionStream('deflate')`; if post-deflate RAM would still exceed `32 MB`, least-recently-visited deflated snapshots spill **losslessly to browser `IndexedDB`** (keyed by `runSeed:roomId`, wiped on death/new run). In private/quota-blocked sessions where `IndexedDB` is unavailable, the cap becomes best-effort (explicitly noted as an exception to the `< 350 MB` gate) with a non-blocking `F3` warning rather than deleting conserved fluid.
* **Target Performance Gate & Determinism-Safe Fallbacks (To Be Benchmarked on 8GB M1 Air via Milestone 2.1 Spike):**
  * *Target Gate:* Locked `60 FPS` (`<= 16.67 ms` total frame = sim `<= 6.0 ms` + render `<= 8.0 ms`, total browser tab process `< 350 MB` outside private-mode no-`IndexedDB`) under full load up to the **`28,672` combined rendered instance cap** (`24,576` cached visible grid fluid + `4,096` ballistic). **How "locked `60 FPS`" is measured everywhere in this doc:** a production build (`vite build && vite preview`, never `vite dev`), Chrome, `F3` overlay's rolling stats over a `30 s` scripted run of the gate's scenario, pass = p95 frame time `<= 16.7 ms` and p99 `<= 25 ms`; memory = Chrome Task Manager "Memory footprint" of the tab at the end of the run. Record the numbers in `docs/perf-log.md` at every gate (the hackathon gate policy is in §10.0).
  * *Rendering-Only Adaptive Fallback (Zero Simulation Divergence):* Triggered when rolling total frame `> 16.0 ms` over 4 frames (recovering `< 14.0 ms`): caps solid chunk remeshes `16 -> 8/frame` and fluid-cache rebuilds `16 -> 8/frame` (both distance-prioritized), skips non-essential cosmetic debris/siphon ribbon detail, and lowers shadow-map update rate—**never modifying simulation tick logic**, keeping `hash32` replays 100% bit-exact across machines.
  * *Explicit Recorded `simLod: 'reduced'` Mode (Optional Replay-Header Flag):* Only active when explicitly selected before a run (and recorded in the replay seed header), deterministically setting `0G` surface-tension to `30 Hz`, conduction to `7.5 Hz`, and max phase explosions to `2/tick`.

## 5. Camera, Orientation & 3D Spatial Readability
* **Hades-Style 3D Isometric Cutaway Camera (~48° Pitch, Fixed World `+Y` Up):**
  * Camera yaw is fixed at `45°` (looking from the South-East corner toward North-West). North & West bulkheads render full height; South & East bulkheads render opaque only up to `0.6 m` above the room's floor top (`y = 2`) and at **`0.15` opacity** above that (viewer-only; core geometry is unchanged).
  * Overhanging catwalk/pillar chunks apply a BatchedMesh fragment-shader **surgical sightline X-ray cutaway** whenever the player, locked-on target, or fluid stream passes beneath them.
* **Gravity-Aligned Suit Orientation & Deck-Relative Plumb-Line:**
  * The camera never rolls. The player's exo-suit aligns `upSign = +1` (boots `-Y`, head `+Y`) in `1G / 0G / 2G / 3G`, and executes a crisp `180°` flip to `upSign = -1` (boots `+Y`, head `-Y`) in `-1G` so magnetic boots stand on the ceiling and underside of catwalks.
  * `plumbDir = -Y` in `1G / 0G / 2G / 3G` and `+Y` in `-1G`. Whenever an entity or `0G` fluid cluster is `> 0.2 m` from a solid surface along `plumbDir`, a laser line casts from `feetY` along `plumbDir` and places a surface ring on the **first solid voxel** hit (hiding cleanly on no-hit). In `0G`, this explicitly acts as a deck-relative altitude indicator.
* **3D Magnetic Target Lock-On:** Snaps a 3D reticle to the nearest **Enemy**, movable **`WOOD_CRATE`**, **`GLASS_VAT`**, or **`0G` fluid cluster** (any `0G` chunk centroid holding `>= 20 L` of fluid) within **`12.0 m`** with unobstructed 3D DDA LOS (passes `GLASS_VAT`/`STEEL_GRATE`, blocked by `HULL_STEEL`/`BOUNDARY_HULL`/`WOOD_BARRIER`/`ICE`). "Nearest" means nearest to the **cursor's aim point** (ties: nearest to the player), re-evaluated every tick, no button needed. Vats are targeted through a `vats[]` registry written by `roomGen` (`{ center, aabb, mat }`, part of `RoomState`); a vat stays targetable while any `GLASS_VAT` voxel remains in its AABB.
* **Aim Model:** the viewer casts the cursor ray into the scene and intersects it with the horizontal plane at the player's torso height (`pos.y + 0.8 m`, mirrored in `-1G`); the aim point is that intersection. Aim direction = unit vector from the player's torso to the locked target's center if a lock exists, else to the aim point. Unlocked aim is therefore horizontal; vertical aim (including the `0G` dash's "3D cursor") comes from a lock on a target above or below the torso. The viewer passes the normalized, `1/1024`-quantized direction to core each tick (§4.1).

## 6. Deterministic Tick Pipeline, Gravity CA, DDA Ballistics & Thermal Rules

### 6.1 Fixed 9-Stage Tick Pipeline (Every `FIXED_DT = 1/60 s`)
Stages 1–8 are the deterministic simulation tick (run `0..3` times per frame by the accumulator); Stage 9 is per-frame render prep in `src/viewer/` and holds no simulation state. **Global write rule:** every Stage 1–8 mutation of a grid cell goes through `writeCell()` / `transitionCell()` / `placeOrDeferFluid()`, which enforce the Grid Write & Snapshot Dirty Contract (§4.4). No system writes `grid[idx]` directly; this is what keeps `dirtySnapshot`, `dirtySolidMesh`, `dirtyDecals`, and `dirtyFluidRender` complete.

1. **Input & Suit State Machine (all suit actions resolve here, before physics):** Process `WASD`, `E`/`Left-Shift`, `Q`/`C`/`V`, `Left-Click`, `Right-Click`, `Space`, `F`, `R`, `X`; tick cooldowns and `C` (`3.0 s`) / `V` (`3.5 s`) timers (starting `GRAV_CD` when `C`/`V` expires or is early-canceled by `Q`). Then execute suit actions in a fixed deterministic order: **(1) Siphon: pick the tick's material (§3.2), run the compatibility check, then—atomically—displacement spill of the old contents or backfire if required, then extraction into the active chamber (a displacement blocked by a full deferred queue extracts nothing that tick), (2) drain any locked/backfiring chamber's pending spill through `placeOrDeferFluid()` (a locked chamber cannot feed output or accept intake until drained), then decay chamber `chargeUnits` by `2`, (3) Nozzle tap/hold quantum spawns, (4) Jetpack Dash exhaust, (5) attachment procs (Spark-Plug, Piezo-Arc, Cryo-Expansion, `3G` Overdrive, Gyro Stabilizer, Ablative Plating)**. Every chamber/tank mutation is atomic inside this stage; no later stage may observe a half-updated tank. Nozzle/dash quanta created here are integrated by Stage 3 on the same tick; siphon removals are visible to Stage 4 on the same tick. `F` airlock interaction only sets `pendingAirlock` here (workbench purchases resolve immediately; if both a terminal and an airlock are in range, the nearer one wins)—the current tick completes simulation Stages 1–8 before `simPaused = true` begins the door cycle.
2. **Entity Kinematics & Collisions:** Integrate `GRAVITY_ACCEL_M_S2` (`1G/2G/3G/-1G/0G`), run swept AABB-vs-voxel collision (`0.2 m` stair step-up) and **entity-entity collision** (resolved deterministically by entity `id` order via positional separation along minimum-penetration axis + 1D elastic momentum exchange `e = 0.4` along collision normal), applying capped Wall-Splat damage (`0.25 s` per-entity i-frames, gated by `entity.impulseTimer > 0`, exempting normal `WASD`/thruster motion and player dash + `0.15 s` grace). A qualifying entity-vs-solid impact also applies **`6` structural damage to `WOOD_BARRIER` and `4` to `ICE` and `GLASS_VAT`** (Tier 1 = `4`, Tier 2 = `6`, Tier 3 = `0`; `3x` when the target is embrittled at `<= -48°C`); `HULL_STEEL`, `STEEL_GRATE`, and `BOUNDARY_HULL` take none. This routes through the structural damage resolver (§6.1 Stage 6) and never bypasses `dirtySolidMesh`/nav invalidation.
3. **Ballistic Quanta Integration & Swept 3D DDA:** Integrate gravity (`1G/2G/3G/-1G`) or `0G` viscous drag (`v *= 0.95/tick`, auto-reinsert at `|v| < 0.35 m/s`), raycast via 3D Amanatides-Woo DDA with strict precedence: **`Entity -> STEEL_GRATE (pass for WATER/OIL/CRYO; solid hit for ICE/ACID) -> Solid -> FIRE/STEAM reaction -> Lighter *ballistic-allowed* Fluid (swap) -> Same/Heavier Fluid (horizontal spread)`**. Only `WATER/OIL/CRYO/ICE/ACID` may enter `ballisticQuanta`; `FIRE` and `STEAM` are never promoted by a swap. A quantum entering `FIRE` resolves by material: `WATER` douses (`FIRE -> AIR`, quantum -> `STEAM` at `lastAirCell` or deferred, `1:1` in `V_water_total`); `OIL` refreshes the existing `FIRE` cell (`timer = 30`, `tempByte = 212`), increments `V_oil_combusted`, and deactivates; `CRYO` boils off (`-> AIR`, `V_cryo_boiled_off++`, deactivates); `ICE` melts `1:1` to `WATER` through `placeOrDeferFluid()` at `lastAirCell` (deactivates); and `ACID` passes through without reaction. A quantum entering `STEAM` passes through without swapping and leaves the gas in place; `STEAM` is never promoted. If a reaction-product placement (`WATER` -> `STEAM` dousing or `ICE` -> `WATER` melting) cannot be accepted by `placeOrDeferFluid()` because the queue is full, the quantum remains active with its pre-reaction material and retries next tick; it is never deactivated without a ledger-counted destination. Ballistic phase transitions (§3.1) are evaluated here, at impact/landing (or at `flightTicks === 2` for `igniteInFlight` quanta). With *Phase-Lock Fuse* rank `>= 1`, a superheated `firedByPlayer` quantum is instead **primed** at impact, and its `fuseTicks` count down here each tick. Primed quanta detonate (apply the §3.1 transition at their cell) at `0`, on the `X` trigger, or early under the rules in §9. Every primed quantum detonates in the last simulated tick before an airlock door cycle starts.
4. **`60 Hz` Fluid & Gas Movement CA:** After the list swap (§4.4), run `1` pass (`1G`/`-1G`/`0G`), `2` sub-passes (`2G`), or `3` sub-passes (`3G`). Sub-pass 1 iterates `activeCells` in **ascending `idx` order when `dirY = -1` and descending order when `dirY = +1`** (so a falling column moves together in both `1G` and `-1G`; `0G` uses ascending); sub-passes 2–3 iterate the destination indices written by the previous sub-pass, in the order they were written (otherwise extra sub-passes would revisit vacated cells and `2G`/`3G` fluid would not fall faster). **Free-fall promotion** is evaluated here per visited liquid cell before Rule 1A: if the `2` cells along `dirY` are `AIR` and a ballistic slot is free, the cell is promoted (§6.2) instead of stepping. **Clear bit 22 (`steppedThisPass`) across all visited cells after every sub-pass** and calling `markCellDirty()` for **both the source and destination cells** of every moved/swapped cell (which sets `dirtySnapshot` and `markFluidRenderDirty` for both chunks). No move may write a source/destination cell directly without this call.
5. **`15 Hz` Cellular Reactions (`(simTick & 3) === 0`) & `1 Hz` Relaxation (`(simTick % 60) === 0`):**
   * (a) Hazard emitter heat/cold injection (emitter definitions in §9), then **`FIRE` neighbor heating**: each `FIRE` cell raises every non-`FIRE` 6-neighbor except `AIR`, `GLASS_VAT`, and `BOUNDARY_HULL` by `+8` bytes (`+32°C`), clamped at `212` (§7). Iteration order for all Stage 5 steps: the fluid list, then the thermal list, each ascending `idx`;
   * (b) **Symmetric 6-Neighbor Conductive-Pair Conduction (`15 Hz`):** Restricted strictly to **conductive–conductive pairs** where both cells belong to `{HULL_STEEL, STEEL_GRATE, WATER, OIL, ICE}` (excluding `AIR`, `CRYO`, `GLASS_VAT`, `WOOD_BARRIER`, `BOUNDARY_HULL`). Each active conductive cell `A` in `activeCells` / `activeThermalSolids` (**the thermal list admits only eligible exposed-surface conductors or solids within `2` voxels of a heat/electric source**) checks **all 6 neighbors `B`**: if `B` is conductive and **`(!isActive(B)) || (idxA < idxB)`** (queried in $O(1)$ via `activeBitset`/`thermalBitset`), compute `deltaBytes = Math.trunc((byteA - byteB) / 8)`. When `deltaBytes !== 0`, apply `byteA -= deltaBytes; byteB += deltaBytes`, **wake/enqueue `B`** into `activeCellsNext` / `activeThermalSolidsNext`, and call **`markCellDirty(idxA)` + `markCellDirty(idxB)`** (both `tempByte` values are persisted bits). Every pair involving at least one active cell executes strictly once with zero orientation bias across active/inactive boundaries, worst-case 6-face drain is `<= 75%`, and `|55 - 50| = 5 < 8` keeps `0°C` `ICE` stable on `+20°C` steel/water while melting next to `>= +32°C` conductors, `FIRE`, or vents *(intentional temperature-only cellular approximation—not energy-conserving specific heat)*;
   * (c) **Pairwise `WATER + FIRE` Dousing:** Requires `steppedThisPass === 0` on both cells; sets both to `1` and appends **both** cells to `stage5Touched`;

### 6.3 Gravity-Aware Movement CA Rules (`60 Hz`)
Let `dirY = -1` (`1G/2G/3G`) and `dirY = +1` (`-1G`):
* **Rule 1A (Liquids — Vertical Step, Grate Permeation & Archimedes Swap):** Liquids (`ACID`, `CRYO`, `WATER`, `OIL`) step along **`+dirY`** into `AIR`, pass straight through `STEEL_GRATE` to the **first non-grate cell beyond the contiguous grate run** along `+dirY` (scanning at most `GRATE_MAX_RUN = 2` cells; every grate in this doc is `0.2 m` = `2` voxels thick, so a fixed `y + 2 * dirY` target would land inside the grate's second layer) if that cell is open/lighter (`WATER`, `OIL`, `CRYO` permeate `STEEL_GRATE`; viscous **`ACID` does not permeate `STEEL_GRATE`**, pooling atop grates to carve them), or swap with strictly lower-density liquids (`ACID > CRYO > WATER > OIL`). **Liquid-into-gas swap:** a liquid stepping along `+dirY` into `STEAM` or `FIRE` cleanly swaps cells—the gas is displaced upward along `-dirY` to the liquid's origin **without being overwritten**—preserving both cells for Stage 5 reactions.
* **Rule 1B (Gases & Plasma — Explicit Upward Buoyancy):** `STEAM` and `FIRE` explicitly rise along **`-dirY`** into `AIR`, pass upward through `STEEL_GRATE` (same contiguous-run rule as Rule 1A, `GRATE_MAX_RUN = 2`), and disperse isotropically via seeded `hash32` 6-neighbor steps in `0G`. **`FIRE` swaps upward through `STEAM`** (since `0.05 < 0.10`), and **`STEAM` bubbles upward by swapping through any liquid directly above it** (`WATER`, `OIL`, `CRYO`, `ACID`) without deleting either cell.
* **Rule 2 (Diagonal Collapse):** Check `(x ± 1, y + dirY, z)` and `(x, y + dirY, z ± 1)` biased by `slideDir`.
* **`N_same` Definition (Canonical):** `N_same(x, y, z, matId)` is the count of same-material cells among the **`26` neighbors** in the `3 x 3 x 3` neighborhood **excluding the center cell** (`0..26`), evaluated with the **moving cell excluded from its origin cell** (the source cell is treated as `AIR` while scoring a candidate move). Rule 3, `0G` surface tension, and every shoreline-cohesion check use this exact function.
* **Rule 3 (Viscosity Dispersion & `3x3x3` Shoreline Cohesion):** Disperse horizontally along `slideDir` (`viscositySteps`: `CRYO = 3`, `WATER = 2`, `ACID = 2`, `OIL = 1`). When no same-material cell occupies the cell directly above (`x, y - dirY, z`), move horizontally only if target `3x3x3` `N_same >= current N_same`.
* **`0G` Surface Tension & Gas Dispersion:** Interior fluid cells (all 6 face neighbors match) early-exit. Surface cells swap into adjacent `AIR` cells that strictly increase `N_same` in `3x3x3` (or equal `N_same` closer to local centroid—the centroid of that material's cells in the cell's chunk, recomputed once per tick for chunks with `0G` fluid). In `0G`, `STEAM` and `FIRE` disperse isotropically via seeded `hash32` 6-neighbor steps.

### 8.2 Combat Math & Tank Volatility Matrix
* **Wall-Splat / Gravity-Slam (Normal Movement Exempt):**
  * *Entity vs. Solid:* `Dmg = min(120, 0.025 * entity.effectiveMassKg * (V_n - 7.5)^2)` for normal impact `V_n > 7.5 m/s` (above `6.5 m/s` run and `5.5 m/s` `0G` cruise; standard `WASD`/thruster motion never triggers self wall-splat).
  * *Entity A vs. Entity B:* Uses the **higher-speed striker's `effectiveMassKg`** with relative closing speed `V_rel > 7.5 m/s`: **`100%` damage to target, `50%` recoil damage to striker**.
  * Both cases multiply by **`3.0x`** (cap `360`) if the damaged entity's `tempByte <= 38` (`-48°C`) and enforce `0.25 s` per-entity `impactIFrames`.
  * **`entity.impulseTimer` Gate (`0.6 s`):** Wall-Splat / Gravity-Slam damage is evaluated only while `entity.impulseTimer > 0`. The timer is set to `0.6 s` by external knockback, Air Burst impulse, explosion impulse, gravity slam, or a fall with `|vy| > 7.5 m/s`; normal `WASD`/thruster motion never sets it, cleanly exempting ordinary movement. It decrements once per `60 Hz` tick. **Own-pulse exemption (player only):** a fall caused by the player's own `C`/`V`/`Q` gravity change (from pulse start until the player next touches ground, or `1.0 s` after the pulse ends) never sets the player's `impulseTimer`—otherwise every `V` would cost `~11` HP (a `3.4 m` fall to the ceiling at `~9.8 m/s`) and a `3G` drop off the `3.8 m` catwalk would deal the full `120` cap and kill the player. Aliens and crates are not exempt.
* **Stun Contract (all entities; Boss values in §8.3 override):** electric shock, a Phase Explosion hit (`f > 0`), or a Wall-Splat dealing `>= 20` dmg stuns for `0.75 s` (`45` ticks) **only if `stunImmunityTicks === 0`**; when a stun ends, `stunImmunityTicks = 120` (`2.0 s`). Without immunity, standing in electrified water would re-stun every tick. The player's *Hazard Lining* (§9) can remove specific stuns and statuses. A stunned alien cannot move or attack and is siphonable (§8.3).
* **Dynamic Crates (`60 kg` dry, `0.06 kg/L` hollow bulk density, `60 HP`, `1.0 x 1.0 x 1.0 m`, `4–12` spawned / `<= 16` cap):** **Dynamic obstacles and projectiles, never walkable platforms**, using full-AABB water-voxel overlap buoyancy (`a_buoy = min(2.2*g, (waterVoxels / 1000) * 2.5*g)`, `0.85/tick` drag, `maxVy = 3.5 m/s`; in `-1G`, buoyancy, `maxVy`, and slam direction flip with `upSign`). Crates ignite on touching `FIRE` or cells `>= +220°C` (`burnTicks = 180` / `3.0 s` at `12 DPS` = `36 dmg` per burn cycle, leaving `24 HP` on a full crate; sets `tempByte = 212` on `1` adjacent `OIL` or `WOOD_BARRIER` cell per `15 Hz` tick (never inert `AIR`), triggering standard Stage 5(e) ignition; extinguished immediately by `WATER`, setting `wetTicks = 120`), deal/take Wall-Splat damage on entity-entity collisions, and **despawn at `0 HP`** (viewer-only splinters; `1` Bio-Cache per combat room and `2` per salvage side-room drop `25` Scrap).
* **Tank Mixing & Backfire:** Siphoning an incompatible fluid into a non-empty **active** chamber displaces the older fluid via expanding BFS around `feetY` before refilling—**except** when incoming `CRYO` meets existing chamber fluid at `>= +100°C` (or incoming `>= +100°C` fluid meets existing `CRYO`), which triggers an immediate **Tank Backfire**. **Which liters spill:** for Cryo Thermal-Shock and electrified-`WATER` backfires, no liter is extracted that tick and the chamber's existing contents spill; for temperature backfires, the tick's liters are extracted and mixed first, then the whole mixed chamber spills. A **Tank Backfire** (`25` self-dmg + mass-scaled `720 kg·m/s` nom. impulse `deltaV = clamp(720 / player.effectiveMassKg, 2.5, 9.0)` + expanding-BFS full chamber spill at `feetY`) also triggers on **any** fill or mix, including an empty-chamber first fill, that results in Starter Tank `WATER` `tempByte >= 75` (`>= +100°C`) or `OIL` `tempByte >= 95` (`>= +180°C`); Baffled chambers are safe through byte `110` (`+240°C` inclusive) and backfire only at `tempByte > 110` (`>= +244°C`, byte `>= 111`) plus cross-material Cryo Thermal Shock. Siphoning electrified `WATER` (`chargeUnits > 0`) into an unbaffled chamber holding a different material also triggers a backfire. The backfire spill drains through `placeOrDeferFluid()` one liter at a time; if ballistic/deferred capacity is exhausted, the remaining liters stay in the **locked** chamber and retry next tick (the chamber cannot accept new fluid until empty), so no liter is deleted.
